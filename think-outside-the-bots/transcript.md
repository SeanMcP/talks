## Survey

I'm looking for some audience participation in this talk. With a show of hands, how many of yinz have worked on building with AI at your current company? [Take note of the percentage] How many of yinz have plans to or have talked about the idea? [Take note of percentage]

So we have about [sum of percentages] of the room that has either built something with AI or would like to. That's a pretty big shift in the last two years when the next generation of models were released by OpenAI.

Before that AI (under the unassuming name of "machine learning") was reserved for special use cases from companies with lots and lots of data to train their models. That is still true today, but we now have access to these highly-trained models through software-as-a-service contracts or by running them on our own hardware (hopefully your graphics card can handle it).

This has triggered an arms race among the largest tech companies to see who can implement the best AI tools the fastest. And that pressure has trickled down to medium and small companies who now need to consider 1) if they should integrate AI and 2) what should they be building.
